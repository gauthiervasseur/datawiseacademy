ABOUT US

Data Wise Academy was founded on a simple idea: transformation succeeds when people, data, and technology advance together. Organizations need more than powerful tools—they need the knowledge, confidence, practical skills, and frameworks to turn data and AI into lasting business impact.

AI is making it easier than ever to imagine, build, and automate. But experimentation alone does not create transformation. Without the right foundations, organizations risk creating a new generation of disconnected solutions—valuable locally, but difficult to maintain, govern, trust, or scale.

At Data Wise Academy, we focus on impact and scale. We empower leaders and teams to identify opportunities, work confidently with data and AI, improve decisions and workflows, and turn ideas into practical business applications. At the same time, we help organizations establish the shared foundations, standards, and capabilities needed to move from individual experimentation to sustainable adoption.

Many can ideate. Few need to develop. Everyone should be able to use.

Our programs combine executive education, practical training, hands-on experimentation, and real business application across data, analytics, AI, GenAI, automation, and emerging technologies. With deep industry experience, global expertise, and Silicon Valley roots, we connect proven foundations with the latest advances in AI—helping organizations build capabilities that are accessible, actionable, trusted, and scalable.

The Data Wise Academy difference: democratizing innovation without democratizing technical debt.

What does Data Wise Academy do?

Data Wise Academy is a Menlo Park, California–based company that trains executives, managers, and analysts to turn data and AI into business impact. We offer seven training programs, applied hackathons, an executive master class series, and Silicon Valley learning expeditions.

Who are your programs for?

Our programs serve three groups: executives and business leaders who sponsor data and AI initiatives; managers and project leaders who run them; and business, BI, and Excel analysts who work with data every day. Each program page lists its intended audience.

How long are the programs?

Training courses last one or two days. Executive sessions last two to three hours. Applied hackathons last one or two days. The Master Class Series includes four to seven sessions, and the Silicon Valley Immersion lasts three to six days in the Bay Area.

Are programs delivered on-site or online?

Programs are delivered on-site at your organization or live online. The Silicon Valley Immersion takes place in the San Francisco Bay Area.

In which languages are programs delivered?

Programs are delivered in English and French. We can arrange real-time translation through simultaneous interpreting at an additional cost, or you can arrange your own.

Can programs be tailored to our organization?

Yes. Every program follows the same proven method, and we adapt examples, exercises, and hackathon challenges to your industry, your data, and your goals. The Silicon Valley Immersion is built around your agenda.

How many people can attend a session?

Up to 40 participants per training course, up to 20 executives per master class, and up to 6 projects per hackathon. Additional projects and AI & Data Facilitators can be added.

What is an applied hackathon, and how do we prepare?

An applied hackathon is a one- or two-day session where your teams solve a real data challenge with their own data, supported by our AI & Data Facilitators, and present the result to leadership. Each team submits a short Problem Brief at least 48 hours in advance, describing the problem, the data involved, the current process, and the goal.

Do participants need technical skills or specific software?

No coding experience is required for executive sessions or master classes. For training courses and hackathons, participants need a laptop. Our approach is vendor-agnostic, so we work with the tools your organization already uses.

What makes your approach different?

Every program combines practical foundations with hands-on application on real data, supported by a dedicated team of AI & Data Facilitators. Teams and leaders share one set of frameworks (VORTEX™, FAIR, TACTICAL™, ARSENAL™, and ISWEAR™), and the approach is vendor-agnostic, so the knowledge lasts beyond any single tool.

Who has Data Wise Academy worked with?

Clients include organizations such as Airbus, Allianz, Nissan, ENGIE, Google, Deloitte, Michelin, L’Oréal, Oracle, and Stanford University, as well as public institutions and business schools.

How much do programs cost?

Pricing depends on the format, duration, and number of participants. Contact us for a proposal.

How do we get started?

Use the contact form or email info@datawiseacademy.com with the program you are interested in, your team size, and your goals. We reply within 24–48 hours.

Another question? Reach out. We reply within 24–48 hours.
